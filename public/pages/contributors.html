Talkomatic Is Built by Its Community

There is no company behind Talkomatic Classic, and no one person who owns it. It is kept running by the people who use it, and it belongs to all of them.

Thank you, all of you

The world's first chat room was written in 1973 by two students who simply thought it would be good if people could talk. Fifty years later it is still here for the same reason: not because anybody is paid to keep it alive, but because enough people care that it does not disappear.

Every person who has sat in a room and talked to a stranger, kept a conversation civil, reported something broken, suggested something better, made a theme, wrote a bot, fixed a bug, or just came back tomorrow, is the reason this exists. This page used to list a handful of names. It was never really a handful of people.

If you have ever typed anything into Talkomatic, this page is about you.

Everyone

The People Who Show Up

A chat room with nobody in it is just a webpage. Everyone who opens a room and starts talking is doing the most important job here, and it is not a small one.

Moderation

Moderators

Volunteers who keep rooms usable without taking them over. They work to rules anyone can read, and they are held to them in public.

Feedback

Bug Reporters and Idea Posters

Most of what has been built here started as somebody saying "this is broken" or "this would be better if". The suggestion board in the lobby is where that happens, and everyone votes.

Design

Theme Makers

People who repaint the whole site and share the result, so nobody has to use it the way it shipped.

Code

Developers

Everyone who has written a line of this, from a one-word typo fix to a whole feature. The source is open so that nobody has to ask permission to help.

Bots

Bot Builders

People who build something odd and useful with the bot editor or the API, and then let everybody else play with it.

History

Everyone Who Came First

The people who wrote Talkomatic in 1973, and everyone who kept a version of it running in the fifty years since. This project stands on all of it.

Want to Contribute?

You do not need to be a developer, and you do not need permission. Post an idea or a bug on the suggestion board in the lobby, make a theme, apply to moderate, or just be someone worth talking to in a room. All of it counts.
